# HART. Mod Pack 1.1.0

HART. Mod Pack now includes all settings, rules and fixes from Mod Pack Patch 0.5.0. One settings page, the retained gameplay defaults and series preset. Unsubscribe from Mod Pack Patch and remove any ModsLocal copy, then restart. If its DLL remains loaded, the pack pauses its gameplay features to prevent duplicate rules. Thriving Rivals, City Performance and the other pack members remain separate required items. Still being playtested.

Supports reviewed RLO 0.50.0, with 0.49.0 support retained. Added “Mute RLO night chirping”, on by default and enabled by Apply HART series settings. It mutes only RLO’s cricket loop; switch it off without restarting.
